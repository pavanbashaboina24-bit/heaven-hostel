# Story

**Ramu** is the first boy from his village (Kothapalli) to reach a city college. His mother sold her bangles for his fees and stitched
him a shirt. At **Heaven Hostel** the boys on his floor laugh at his accent, hide his books, lock him out of the terrace (the only place
his phone has a signal). One night in **Room 204** he takes his own life. The warden files a report and nobody says sorry.

The hostel is locked in time. You arrive to find out why. His ghost walks the halls. Ten puzzles are the pieces of his story.
The last candle opens a hidden door; behind it is his secret room, and a bed that has been waiting for the letter he never sent.

Tone: sad, not gory. The ending is about being *asked how you are*.

# HEAVEN HOSTEL - SPOILERS (9 puzzles + back stairs + secret ending room)

Notes, cards and items move to different rooms in every new game, and the radio number,
fuse switches, book colour etc. change too. The rules below always stay the same.

**Exact answers for the game you are playing right now:** press F12, open the Console, type

    hhSpoiler()

and press Enter. It prints every answer and every room.
(The game's own hints also say which room holds each note, after you solve the previous puzzle.)

## CHAPTER 1 - "What happened to Ramu?"  (4 puzzles, any order)

| # | Puzzle | Where | How to solve |
|---|--------|-------|--------------|
| 1 | Drawer code | Reception desk (warden's note) + Room 204 desk (name list) | Code = **204** + number of **crossed-out** names. 2 crossed out -> **2042** |
| 2 | Trunk word lock | Trunk at the foot of the bed, Room 204 | Notebook text is every letter **one step forward**. Move each letter ONE BACK (B->A). Answer = the nickname after "THEY CALLED ME" (e.g. UIFZ DBMMFE NF CVNQLJO -> BUMPKIN) |
| 3 | Bookcase | Mess hall (ground-floor hall), south wall | The warden's note names a colour. Press E on the book of **that colour**. A hidden door at the bottom opens |
| 4 | Fuse box | Terrace room, beside the door | Card 1 + card 2 list 3 switches that must be **UP**. All others DOWN. Press Enter to pull the lever |

## CHAPTER 2 - "What does Ramu want?"  (5 puzzles, the candles come last)

| # | Puzzle | Where | How to solve |
|---|--------|-------|--------------|
| 5 | Radio | Reception desk | Anna FM number is on the station card; the draft letter says Old Gold = Anna FM **+ N**. Tune to Anna + N |
| 6 | Key board | Behind the reception desk | Third floor rooms are 301-304 from the stairs. Gopal sleeps in the Nth room from the stairs -> **30N** (2nd room = 302) |
| 7 | Rat hole | Mess hall, north wall, near the floor | Pick up the **cheese** (in a random room, see the hint). Stand at the hole, press E. The rat takes it, a brick falls out |
| 8 | Lever | Terrace room, back wall, left of the altar | Pick up the **iron handle** (random room, see the hint). Press E at the lever to fit it, press E again to pull. A hidden cupboard opens |
| 9 | Candles | Altar, terrace room | Needs puzzles 5-8 done first. The 3 diary pages list names as 1st-7th. Press the candle number whose name is 1st, then 2nd ... 7th |

After puzzle 9 you get the rusty blade and Ramu's last wish -> go to the swing in the yard.

## BACK STAIRS TO THE TERRACE (optional way up)
- Behind the hostel (west side of the yard, beyond the graveyard fence) stands a round iron staircase inside a cage with a **locked gate**.
- **The key:** an old iron key lies on the floor of one random room (new room each game). After puzzle 4 (the terrace photograph) the clue page tells you which room. `hhSpoiler()` also prints it.
- Unlock the gate (E). When you step inside, the gate slams shut behind you and a message says the hostel is locked until Ramu's story is finished.
- Climb the stairs: hold W and they carry you round (S goes back down). At the top a short bridge leads onto the terrace.

## PAPERS VANISH
- When a puzzle is solved, its papers disappear: the notes lying in the rooms (and any you picked up) are gone, and the warden's note on the reception desk too. You keep the clue page you earned.
- So read and use a note BEFORE you solve its puzzle.

## FALLING
- The terrace and floors now end at the outer walls. Jump from the roof of the terrace room over the parapet and you fall.
- A drop of more than 6 metres kills ("You fell ... metres"). One floor (4 m) is safe.
- Walking off the edge on foot is still blocked (only a jump gets you over).

## THE SECRET ENDING ROOM  (door not visible? F12 console: hhReveal() shows it, hhRoom() goes in)
- After the 9 puzzles you get the blade + last wish, cut the swing and find **Ramu's letter**.
- Then the hostel shudders and a **hidden door** appears in the mess hall (ground-floor hall), on the **north wall, directly OPPOSITE the bookcase** (the bookcase is on the south wall). It does not exist before this moment.
- Press E on the door: the screen fades and you are in **Ramu's secret room** (far underground). The ghost cannot follow you here.
- Press E on the **bed** with the letter in your bag: the ending plays.

## GHOST CHANGES (quick reference)
- Patrols the whole hostel room by room, floor by floor (ground, 1st, 2nd, terrace, then back down), slowly. It never stands waiting.
- It only attacks after it SEES you (from far away and from any side). Slow when far, fast when close.
- Can walk, appear and stop on stairs; a re-plan in the middle of a flight no longer sticks it.
- The stair torches (fire) are now wall tube lights.
